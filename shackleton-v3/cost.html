Investment

Shackleton Rover is conceived as a lightweight lunar expedition designed to deliver meaningful scientific, technological, and strategic outcomes at comparatively low cost. The mission will demonstrate how carefully targeted robotic exploration can provide substantial national capability without requiring the scale or expense of flagship international programmes.

We estimate the required investment to be £25 million across the 2029/2030 funding period. This represents exceptional value for money in exchange for the advances in national capability, scientific return, and strategic presence.

Our team is currently working up to a Rover Requirements Definition Meeting, to be held in October 2026. At the end of this meeting, the requirements will be passed to a suitably qualified and experienced cost engineer. This contractor will be tasked to create an independent schedule and budget for the mission.

We will then seek substantive funding to deliver this mission.
