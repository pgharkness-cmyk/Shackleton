Investment

Shackleton Rover is conceived as a focused, lightweight lunar expedition designed to deliver meaningful scientific, technological, and strategic outcomes at comparatively low cost. The mission demonstrates how carefully targeted robotic exploration can provide substantial national capability without requiring the scale or expense of flagship international programmes.

By concentrating on a tightly defined task, the mission seeks to maximise scientific and strategic return while remaining financially realistic.

With this in mind, the total proposed mission cost is approximately £25 million across the 2029/2030 funding period. In strategic terms, and in the context of the potential achievement, the mission represents exceptional value for money in the context of national capability, scientific return, and strategic presence.
