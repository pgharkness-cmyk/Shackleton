The Investment

Shackleton Rover is designed as a focused, lightweight lunar expedition capable of delivering meaningful scientific, technological, and strategic outcomes at comparatively low cost. The mission seeks to demonstrate that small national programmes can achieve disproportionate influence through carefully targeted exploration architectures.

The proposed mission cost is approximately £22 million over the 2029/2030 funding period: less than one percent of the planned UK Space Agency budget during the same timeframe.
