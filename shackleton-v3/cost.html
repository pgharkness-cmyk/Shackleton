The Investment

Shackleton Rover is designed as a focused, lightweight lunar expedition capable of delivering meaningful scientific, technological, and strategic outcomes at comparatively low cost.

The mission architecture deliberately prioritises simplicity, low mass, commercial delivery opportunities, and the reuse of existing technologies wherever possible. By concentrating on a tightly defined scientific objective, the expedition seeks to maximise national impact while remaining financially realistic within the context of the UK civil space budget.

The proposed mission cost is approximately £22 million over the 2029/2030 funding period: less than one percent of the planned UK Space Agency budget during the same timeframe.
