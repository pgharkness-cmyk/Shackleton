A new world

The Shackleton Rover is proposed as an ambitious, inspirational, and strategic national endeavour.

We have a window of opportunity during which we can deliver a global scientific achievement, position ourselves for the lunar economy, and shape the regulation of space in the best traditions of the Antarctic Treaty System. We have unique tools that will allow us to search for water-ice from a smaller platform than our competitors. And in the process, we will drive engagement and excitement by leveraging clear parallels with the heroic age of polar exploration.

We will boost national morale, solidify our capabilities, protect the lunar environment, and spark new careers in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.

We ask you to come with us.
