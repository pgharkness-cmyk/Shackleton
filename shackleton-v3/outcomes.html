Shackleton Rover is intended not merely as a scientific mission, but as a strategic national endeavour capable of generating long-term benefits across science, engineering, education, industry, and international collaboration.

The mission seeks to demonstrate that ambitious exploration objectives can be pursued through focused, technically credible, and comparatively low-cost architectures. Beyond the immediate scientific objectives, the programme aims to strengthen UK capability in robotic exploration, lunar operations, regolith interaction, instrumentation, and emerging in-situ resource utilisation technologies.

Further material on scientific outcomes, partnerships, skills development, economic impact, and long-term strategic benefits will be added here as the programme develops.
