Mission Overview

Shackleton Rover is a proposed British-led lunar exploration initiative focused on robotic investigation of the Moon’s south polar region. The mission concept combines scientific exploration, engineering development, and international stewardship principles inspired by the history of Antarctic exploration.

The initiative aims to investigate water ice deposits trapped within permanently shadowed regions near Shackleton Crater, supporting future understanding of in-situ resource utilisation and long-duration exploration architectures.
