Spark

This will be the first British mission to the surface of the Moon. Inspired by Sir Ernest Shackleton's spirit of discovery, it will focus national capability around a clear scientific goal, ensure our access to the emerging lunar economy, and spark new careers at the very edge of the possible.

⚗

Science

Seeking to make first contact with water ice within the permanently shadowed regions of the lunar south pole, Shackleton Rover will help determine how free hydrogen and oxygen might be created on the Moon. These new propellants will support the future exploration of the solar system.

⬡

Stewardship

Building on the principles of the Antarctic Treaty System, our presence on the ground will position the UK for a consultative role in the regulation of the Moon as a New Antarctica: a global commons with scientific cooperation, responsible exploration, and environmental protections.
